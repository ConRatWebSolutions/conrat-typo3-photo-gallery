====================
ConRat Photo Gallery
====================

:Extension key:

:Rendered:

----

Image galleries for TYPO3 14 that editors maintain in one place – a backend
module with drag-and-drop upload and sorting – and that content elements show
anywhere on the website, in four layouts with responsive images and an
accessible lightbox.

----

**Live demo:** https://demotypo14.conrat-projects.de/fotogalerie

**Product page:** https://somesolutions.de/typo3-bildergalerie/
